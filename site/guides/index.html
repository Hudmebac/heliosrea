New to the room?

Start with the three audience routes: watch the narrated explainer, read one fact file, or go straight to the dossier. Then use the guides in order: scope → evidence → redaction → verification → story → video → publish → field notes → support.

No exploit content

These guides cover evidence, repair and publication on authorised targets. They do not teach control bypass, credential theft or unauthorised access.

What every guide assumes

  • You own the target or have written permission to inspect it.
  • You will redact secrets and third-party data before publishing.
  • You will state the limits, including the probes that returned nothing.
  • You will not invent a result, a customer quote or a revenue claim.
