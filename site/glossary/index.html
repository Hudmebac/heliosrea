Glossary

The words the room uses, without the fog.

A short shared vocabulary for readers, operators and experts. If a term is doing more work than the evidence can support, it is probably hiding a weak claim.

terms 15level plain languagelinks fact files and method

Dossier

The local record of an investigation: target, scope, authorisation, probes, findings, seams, hashes and limits.

Finding

One recorded observation with an ID, family, title, locator and confidence. A finding is not automatically a vulnerability.

Fact file

A stable public page that expands one finding or seam, with the locator, a verification path and the limit.

Locator

The exact file, line, request, probe result or inventory scope that supports a claim.

Confidence

A recorded judgement about the strength of an observation, not a probability and not a truth score.

Change surface

A place where behaviour can be altered or repaired. Also called a seam. It is not a claim that a change was made.

Seam

The short name for a change surface: source, configuration, protocol, binary, edge content or firmware.

Evidence ladder

The outside-in probe sequence: identify the surface, read manifests and docs, map code or requests, then inspect only when justified.

Publication gate

The check that refuses unauthorised, synthetic, thin or fixture-based dossiers before a public page can be generated.

Appendix

The full evidence record, including test-only observations, duplicates, empty probes and errors. The story is a selection; the appendix is the audit trail.

Digest

A SHA-256 hash of the dossier. It is a receipt that the public file came from a specific record, not a proof that every claim is correct.

Redaction

Replacing secret-shaped or private values at the source while keeping the finding, locator and limit. A blur is not a redaction.

Paper edit

A shot script written from the dossier before recording. It names what to show, not what to claim.

Probe status

Signal, empty, error, skipped or retry. The status is part of the evidence and must stay visible.

Operator

The named human who owns the target or has written permission, sets the stop condition and signs off publication.
