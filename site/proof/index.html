What a testimonial must include

  • The person's name and role, or a documented reason for anonymity.
  • Written permission to publish the quote and the attribution.
  • The date and the work it refers to.
  • A link to the evidence file, guide or delivered output.
  • Whether the person paid, and whether anything was discounted.
  • The original wording, with edits disclosed.

How a future quote gets published

  1. Deliver the work. A paid guide or a scoped teardown with a real output.
  2. Ask for a specific quote. What changed, what was useful and what was not.
  3. Get written permission. Name, role, date and the exact wording.
  4. Link the proof. Add the relevant evidence file or delivery note.
  5. Publish or decline. No editing to sound more enthusiastic; no anonymous five-star cards.

Public submission is not open yet. Quotes are collected manually so the attribution and permission can be checked. If you are a buyer with a real experience, the operator can add it through the private Engine Room.
