Privacy and control

The form stores your email, optional first name, update choices, consent time and source in a local JSON file outside the public site. It does not collect an IP address or use an analytics script. To leave, reply to a note or email mysurnameandfirsname@gmail.com.

Questions

How often will it arrive?

The aim is one note a month. If there is no new evidence, the room does not invent an issue. The archive shows what has actually been marked ready.

Do I need to donate?

No. The field notes are free and separate from the optional £5 donation.

Can I choose only new evidence files?

Yes. The form lets you choose monthly field notes, new evidence files, or both.

Is the email sent automatically?

No. This local build stores the list and drafts the issue; the owner reviews and sends it from the contact address. That is stated here instead of pretending an email service exists.
