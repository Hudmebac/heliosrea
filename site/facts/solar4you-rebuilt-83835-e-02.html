What was recorded

Reimplement or intercept the client protocol at the request boundary; keep it read-only until authorised.

Where it sits

The seam is recorded at http://www.w3.org/2000/svg. It is a place where behaviour can be altered or repaired; it is not a claim that a change was made.

Safe way to test it

Take a baseline on a copy or a maintenance window, make the smallest reversible change, and compare the same probes before and after. Record the result even if nothing changes.

What was not done

No change is claimed by this fact file. The risk and confidence are the dossier's assessment, not a guarantee that the seam is safe in production.

A seam is a place where change is possible. It is not a change that was made or a proven exploit.
