Fact files

One claim per page, with the receipt

A fact file is the smallest useful unit of the evidence room. It takes one finding or one change surface and shows what was recorded, why it matters, how to verify it and what it does not prove.

fact files 18groups 3source published dossiers

What you will find

The exact observation, the source locator, the confidence, the related seam and a plain-language limit. No new claim is introduced.

What you will not find

No invented exploit, no score out of ten, no anonymous customer quote and no “trust me” summary. If the dossier did not record it, the fact file does not say it.

How to read a fact file

  1. Read the claim. It should be one sentence you can check.
  2. Follow the locator. Open the file, line or probe result named on the page.
  3. Check the confidence. A direct observation is stronger than an inference.
  4. Read the limit. It tells you what the observation does not establish.
