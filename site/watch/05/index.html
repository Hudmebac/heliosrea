The empty probe is evidence

Signal, empty, error, skipped, retry. The status is part of the finding. This note explains why a clean ledger includes the rows that found nothing.

format narrated MP4voice Susan · calmbase en-GBsource REA-20261121-afd06c73-83835

What this note covers

  • The five probe statuses.
  • Why empty and error rows stay visible.
  • How the ledger limits the story.

What it is not

  • No claim that an empty probe is a failure.
  • No claim that the ledger is complete beyond its scope.
  • No exploit path or hidden success.

Read the transcript

01
Thirteen probes.

Thirteen probes, ten records, seven seams, and one ledger that keeps the status of every row.

02
Signal is not the only result.

Signal is not the only result. A useful observation is just one status in the ladder.

03
Empty is evidence.

Empty is evidence. The probe ran and found nothing to report. That changes what a reader should believe.

04
Error is evidence.

An error is evidence too. Record the failure, then decide whether a retry is safe.

05
Skipped is evidence.

Skipped is evidence. Sometimes the right move is to leave the probe alone and say why.

06
Retry needs a reason.

A retry needs a reason. Transient failure, same scope, written down.

07
Do not hide the ledger.

Do not hide the ledger. The empty and error rows travel with the public file.

Read the whole ladder.

Read the whole ladder. The gaps are part of the claim.
