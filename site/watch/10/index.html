What the room refuses

No exploits. No borrowed targets. No invented testimonials. No paid pack. The refusals are not a disclaimer at the bottom; they decide what can be published.

format narrated MP4voice Susan · lightbase en-GBsource REA-20261121-afd06c73-83835

What this note covers

  • The room's public refusals.
  • The donation-only support model.
  • The limits that travel with every file.

What it is not

  • No exploits, stolen data or control bypass.
  • No customer testimonial or sales record.
  • No paid pack, subscription or promised outcome.
