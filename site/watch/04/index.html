A seam is a place, not a change

The source is the first change surface. The safe path is baseline, edit one module, compare. This note explains what E-01 records and what it still does not prove.

format narrated MP4voice George · closebase en-GBsource REA-20261121-afd06c73-83835

What this note covers

  • What a change surface means.
  • The baseline, edit and compare path.
  • The confidence, risk and limit attached to E-01.

What it is not

  • No claim that a change was made.
  • No claim that a repair is safe.
  • No runtime claim beyond the recorded probes.

01
A seam is a place, not a change.

A seam is a place, not a change. It is somewhere behaviour could change.

02
The first seam is the source.

The first seam is the source. In a tree we own, a direct edit is possible.

03
Take a baseline.

Take a baseline first. Record the clean state before touching anything.

04
Change one module.

Change one module. The smallest change gives the clearest comparison.

05
Compare the result.

Then compare the result. The diff is the evidence, not the intention.

06
Ninety percent. Low risk.

The seam carries ninety percent confidence and a low risk label. That is a recorded judgement, not a guarantee.

07
A seam is not a repair.

A seam is not a repair. It does not claim the change was made, or that the result is safe.

What it does not prove.

And the limit stays. No runtime claim beyond the probe, and no client work.
