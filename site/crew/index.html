How the lanes hand off

  1. Evidence → Verification. The dossier, locators and raw observations move to the fact-checker. Nothing is public yet.
  2. Verification → Story and Video. The claim-to-evidence table becomes the editorial spine and the shot list. Limits travel with both.
  3. Story and Video → Publish and Share. The package is generated, link-checked and only then offered next to the optional donation and the field-note signup.
