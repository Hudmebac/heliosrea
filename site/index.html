What this is

A working evidence room

A place to open an owned device, app or workflow, map what is actually there and show the seam where a repair, rebuild or better design becomes possible. The public file is generated from a local dossier, not written from memory.

The room includes the evidence library, per-finding fact files, long-form field guides, the publication method, the crew roles and a proof page that says what is known and what is not.

What this is not

Not a hacking shop

No exploits, stolen data, control bypasses, borrowed credibility or five-star quotes with no name attached. No “review” unit we do not own. No promise that a teardown will produce a finding or that a buyer will appear.

If the evidence is thin, the gate blocks it. If a testimonial does not exist, the page says so. If a probe returned nothing, the limit stays visible.

  1. Scope. Name the owner, the permission, the depth and the stop condition.

  4. Package. Generate the story, evidence appendix, fact files, video script, card and hash.

Questions

Before you go deeper

Is this a hacking site?

No. The room only inspects targets the operator owns or has written permission to inspect. It publishes evidence, repairs and design seams, not exploits or bypass steps.

Why is there only one evidence file?

The publication gate refuses thin, synthetic or unauthorised dossiers. One real file is more useful than twenty mock ones. New files appear only when a real investigation is complete.

Are the testimonials real?

There are no customer testimonials yet. The proof page uses a real project brief, a recorded dossier verdict and an editorial rule, each labelled. It will publish a customer quote only with a name, permission and a link to the delivered work.

What is a fact file?

A stable page for one finding or change surface. It gives the observation, the locator, the confidence, a verification path and the limit. It is not a marketing summary.

Is there a paid pack?

No. There is no paid pack. There is one optional £5 donation link. The evidence, fact files, method and field guides are free to read.

How do I get updates?

Use the monthly field-note form. It is opt-in, asks only for an email address and an optional first name, and stores the request locally. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Are the field notes finished?

There are ten finished short narrated captioned field notes generated from the real dossier. They use three local Windows OneCore voices in ten presenter treatments. There is no recorded teardown video and no client footage. The notes are a door into the evidence, not a replacement for it.

The rules

What the room will not do

  • Only open devices, apps and accounts you own or have written permission to inspect.
  • Redact serial numbers, keys, customer data and anything that identifies a third party.
  • Do not publish exploits, bypass steps or instructions that defeat a control.
  • Do not buy review units, stock footage, ads or software to start. Use what you already own.
  • No revenue is guaranteed. The first sale comes from a real buyer and delivered work.
  • No dossier, no publication. A story must be built from a completed, authorised investigation.
