Method

Evidence first. Story second. Sale last.

The room has one job: turn an authorised investigation into a public file that a sceptical reader can check. This page is the operating method behind every guide, fact file and story.

The promise

Every public claim has a finding ID, a locator, a confidence and a limit. The page is generated from a dossier, not written from memory.

The refusal

No fixture, demo, example host or private network capture is published automatically. A thin dossier stays in the Engine Room.

The five stages

1. Scope

Name the target, owner, permission, depth, data handling and stop condition. The scope is one page and it travels with the dossier. If the permission is unclear, the run does not start.

2. Evidence

Run the ladder from the outside in. Record signal, empty, error, skipped and retry. Choose the next probe from the result, not from a tool list. Keep the raw locator for every finding.

3. Verify

Map the claim to the evidence. Check duplicates, fixture data and absolute paths. Redact secrets and private data at the source. Read the appendix like an adversary.

4. Package

The studio writes the story, fact files, evidence appendix, video script, card and manifest. The public story selects production evidence; the appendix keeps the full record.

5. Publish and share

Put the static folder online only after the gate and a link check. Offer the optional £5 donation and the opt-in field notes next to the proof. There is no paid pack and no promised outcome.

What the publication gate checks

  • Operator attested ownership or written permission.
  • Investigation status is complete.
  • Target is not a fixture, demo, example domain or private network capture.
  • At least three evidence records and one mapped change surface.
  • Findings retain an ID, locator and confidence; secrets are redacted.

Probe statuses

StatusWhat it meansWhat to do next
signalA usable observation with a locator.Map it to a finding and a confidence.
emptyThe probe ran and found nothing to report.Keep it visible; write the next question.
errorThe probe failed.Record the error; retry only if it is safe.
skippedDeliberately not run.Say why, especially if the network was disabled.
retryAttempted again after a transient failure.Record the reason and the final result.

Confidence is a judgement

RangeHow to read itPublication use
90–100%Direct observation with a strong locator.Can lead a story if the limit is stated.
75–89%Strong evidence, possibly one inference.Use with corroboration and a clear locator.
60–74%Useful but incomplete.Supporting evidence, not the headline.
Below 60%Weak or heavily inferred.Keep in the appendix until another probe confirms it.

What the room refuses

Targets

  • Devices, accounts or systems without ownership or written permission.
  • Production systems without a maintenance window and a rollback.
  • Private WiFi/LAN captures or personal data as public content.
  • Example domains, fixtures, demos and synthetic dossiers.

Content

  • Exploit steps, credential theft or control bypass.
  • Invented findings, market sizes or customer quotes.
  • Absolute local paths or test fixture URLs in a public story.
  • Guaranteed income, guaranteed findings or fake urgency.

Glossary

TermMeaning
DossierThe local record of an investigation: target, scope, probes, findings, seams and limits.
FindingOne recorded observation with an ID, family, title, locator and confidence.
Change surfaceA place where behaviour can be altered or repaired. A seam, not a change that was made.
Fact fileA public page expanding one finding or seam, with verification and limits.
GateThe publication check that refuses unauthorised, synthetic or thin work.
AppendixThe full evidence record, including test-only observations and duplicates.
Paper editA shot script built from the dossier before any video is recorded.
ManifestThe publication metadata and hash that identifies the generated file.
The honest limit: the method reduces slop; it does not make an investigation complete. A confident page can still be wrong. The locator and the limit are there so a reader can check.
