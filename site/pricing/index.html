Questions

Is the £5 a pack?

No. There is no paid pack. The £5 link is a one-time donation with no deliverable attached.

Is this a subscription?

No. The donation is one-time. The field notes are a separate opt-in list and do not require a donation.

Will I be added because I donated?

No. The Stripe donation and the field-note form are separate. Only the form, with a ticked consent box, adds you to the list.

How do I unsubscribe?

Reply to any field note or email mysurnameandfirsname@gmail.com. You can also ask the owner to pause or remove your address.

Can I get a refund?

Donations are voluntary. If a payment went wrong, use the Stripe receipt or contact the room. No refund policy is invented on this page.

Do you take on any target?

No. Any inspection must be owned or authorised, the scope must be safe and the question must be answerable without exploiting a third party.

No income claim: the room is new. The proof page states that no customer testimonial or sales record is published yet. There is no claim that a donation will produce a finding or an income.
