Authorised capability range

Start simple. Earn every next step.

Four stages from a passive look at your own WiFi to encrypted-system analysis. Each stage states the authorisation it needs, the probes it allows, the evidence to capture and the point where you stop. A blocked control is a finding, never a challenge to defeat.

Authorisation is the first control. Only targets you own or have written permission to assess. No stealth, trace erasure, anti-forensics, credential replay or control bypass. Legality is permission, not the absence of a trace. The dossier records the scope and attestation; that record is evidence, not something to hide. If you cannot name the owner, stop.

The range is deliberately conservative. Stage 1 is passive by default; the LAN device sweep requires a separate tick. Stages 2–4 are static analysis and documentation first. A control that stops you is recorded as evidence — never bypassed, evaded or hidden.
